Lisa Marangon

Swim teacher since she was 17, and a former professional triathlete.

I learned to swim properly at 13 and got fast very quickly. I was always an active kid and good at every sport I tried. In my HSC year my local squad coach asked if I'd like to teach swimming, and at 17 my career began. I started with children, then moved on to coaching adults as my experience grew. I practise what I preach.

I'm passionate about getting the best out of every swimmer, child or adult, at any level. To this day I still get goosebumps: nothing makes me happier than sharing what I know and helping people find the best in themselves. I raced triathlon at a high level for several years. Now I focus on helping others reach their swimming goals.
