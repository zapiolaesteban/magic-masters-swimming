Private Lessons

Private swimming lessons are open all year round for all levels and ages – from total beginners to those looking to improve their technique or learn a new stroke or as well as advanced swimmers seeking specific tips.

1-1 sessions cater for exactly what you need. I focus on form and body movement to swim injury free and to help you feel more comfortable in the water on any stroke.

Lessons start from $65 for a unique 30 min session to $235 for a 4 Pass Private pack. Please contact me to secure your spot.

Note: Private Sessions are subject to availability.

Lisa Marangon

Learning to properly swim at the age of 13 years old I become fast very quickly. I was always an active child and was good at all sports I competed in. Whilst in my HSC year my local squad coach had asked if I was interested in being a swim teacher and my career had start at the age of 17 years old. I started with children and as my experience progressed I achieved helping adults. Gaining experience and knowledge and practicing what I preach.

I’m passionate on achieving the best out of any person (child or adult) at any level to give them my knowledge and help them achieve greatness. To this day I still get goose bumps and my happiness comes from sharing knowledge and helping people to find the best in themselves. I competed at a high level in Triathlon for several years but now focus on helping others to achieve their swimming goals.

Career in triathlon

  • 2003 Ironman world champs 2nd 18-24
  • 2004 Ironman world champs 3rd 18-24
